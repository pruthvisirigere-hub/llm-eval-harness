# Blue Peak Coffee Roasters — Knowledge Base

## About

Blue Peak Coffee Roasters was founded in 2018 in Portland, Oregon by Maya Chen and Jordan Okafor. The company started as a single market stall and now roasts all of its coffee in small weekly batches. Blue Peak's mission is to make specialty coffee approachable without being pretentious.

## Locations

Blue Peak has three store locations. The flagship store is in Portland, Oregon, open daily from 7am to 6pm. The second store is in Seattle, Washington, open daily from 7am to 5pm. The third store is in Austin, Texas, open daily from 8am to 6pm. All three locations serve espresso drinks and sell whole-bean coffee.

## Events

Blue Peak hosts public coffee cuppings on Saturdays at 10am at the Portland flagship store. Cuppings are free, last about 45 minutes, and no booking is needed. The Portland store also runs a beginner brewing class on the first Sunday of every month.

### Summit Blend

The Summit Blend is Blue Peak's signature medium roast, made from beans from Ethiopia and Colombia. It tastes of red apple, caramel, and cocoa. A 12oz bag costs $18. It works well for both pour-over and drip machines.

### Midnight Dark

Midnight Dark is Blue Peak's darkest roast, made from beans from Sumatra and Brazil. It tastes of dark chocolate, molasses, and smoke. A 12oz bag costs $20. It is best brewed as espresso or in a French press.

### Sunrise Light

Sunrise Light is a light roast made from a single origin in Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia. It tastes of peach, jasmine, and honey. A 12oz bag costs $19. It is best brewed as pour-over.

### Moonlight Decaf

Moonlight Decaf is Blue Peak's decaf coffee, made from Colombian beans using the sugarcane decaf process. It tastes of milk chocolate and hazelnut. A 12oz bag costs $18. It is available as whole bean or ground.

### Cold Brew Cans

Blue Peak sells ready-to-drink cold brew in 12oz cans for $4.50 each. The cans are brewed from the Summit Blend and steeped for 18 hours. They are sold in all three stores and online.

## Brewing Guide

For pour-over, Blue Peak recommends a coffee-to-water ratio of 1:16, which means 1 gram of coffee for every 16 grams of water. Use water heated to 200°F and a medium-fine grind size. Bloom the grounds with a small amount of water for 30 seconds, then pour slowly in circles until you reach your target weight. Total brew time should be about 3 minutes.

## Subscriptions

The Blue Peak coffee subscription costs $16 per month for one 12oz bag, with your choice of roast. Orders over $35 ship free anywhere in the United States. Subscribers can pause anytime from their account page, skip a month, or cancel with no fee. Subscription boxes ship on the first Monday of each month.

## Returns

Blue Peak offers 30-day returns on unopened bags of coffee. To start a return, email hello@bluepeak.example with your order number and you will receive a prepaid return label. Opened bags cannot be returned, but if a bag arrives damaged Blue Peak will replace it free of charge.

## Loyalty

Peak Points is Blue Peak's loyalty program. Customers earn 1 point for every $1 spent in store or online. Once you reach 100 points you get a $10 reward to spend on anything in the store. Points never expire, and members also get early access to limited seasonal roasts.

## Sustainability

All Blue Peak coffee bags are compostable, and the company uses carbon-neutral shipping on every order. Blue Peak donates 1% of revenue to reforestation projects in coffee-growing regions. The roastery runs on 100% renewable electricity.

## Wholesale

Blue Peak offers wholesale coffee in 5lb bags for cafes and offices. Wholesale pricing starts at $55 per 5lb bag with a minimum order of four bags. To set up a wholesale account, email wholesale@bluepeak.example.
